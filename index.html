A calm, dense look

Surfaces before boxes

Side panels are borderless and a shade lighter than the canvas.

Borders mean structure

Space and background separate regions. Rules only where needed.

Colour means state

Blue is focus, yellow is attention, green is ready.

Dense, not cramped

One-cell gaps. Tables may be tight; controls get room.


Light and dark are both first-class. The theme follows the terminal's real background, and card fills are that background tinted toward a role hue, so they suit any terminal.
